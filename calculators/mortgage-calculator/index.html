How does a mortgage calculator work?

A mortgage calculator estimates the monthly principal and interest payment for a fixed-rate home loan. You enter the home price, down payment, interest rate, and loan term, and the calculator estimates the payment required to repay the loan over the selected term.

Mortgage payment formula

The standard fixed-rate mortgage payment formula is:

M = P × [r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1]

P represents the loan principal, r represents the monthly interest rate, and n represents the total number of monthly payments.

What is included in a mortgage payment?

A basic mortgage calculation estimates principal and interest. Actual housing costs can also include property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, HOA fees, and other expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much will my monthly mortgage payment be?

Your monthly payment depends mainly on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment term. Enter your numbers above to calculate an estimate.

Does the down payment affect the mortgage payment?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the amount borrowed, which generally reduces the principal and interest payment.

Does this calculator include property taxes and insurance?

No. This basic calculator estimates principal and interest. Taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance, and other housing costs may need to be added separately.

How does an auto loan calculator work?

An auto loan calculator estimates the regular payment for a fixed-rate vehicle loan. Enter the vehicle price, down payment, annual percentage rate (APR), and loan term. The calculator then estimates the amount financed and applies the standard fixed-payment loan formula.

Auto loan payment formula

For a standard amortizing loan, the monthly payment is based on the principal, monthly interest rate, and total number of monthly payments. A larger down payment reduces the principal being financed.

What can change your car payment?

Vehicle price, down payment, APR, and loan length all affect the estimated payment. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment but may increase the total interest paid. Taxes, registration, dealer fees, trade-in credits and other costs can also change the actual amount financed.

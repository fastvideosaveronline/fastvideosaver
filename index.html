US Mortgage Equations

Fixed mortgage calculations rely on standard compounding annuities:
M = P [ r(1+r)^n ] / [ (1+r)^n – 1 ].
Early amortization phases primarily service interest charges, gradually shifting weight toward principal balance reductions as the term progresses.

Mifflin-St Jeor Metabolic Model

Basal Metabolic Rate calculates baseline daily caloric consumption:
BMR (Men) = (10 × wt) + (6.25 × ht) – (5 × age) + 5.
Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) scales baseline BMR by physical activity coefficients to support precise weight management plans.

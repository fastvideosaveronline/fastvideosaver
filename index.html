US Mortgage Amortization Dynamics

Fixed-rate home loans follow the standard standard compounding annuity formula:
M = P [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n – 1 ]. Early loan cycles allocate majority funds toward interest balance reduction rather than equity ownership. Our tool models principal compression clearly over 15 to 30 year timelines.

The Exponential Engine of Compound Growth

Compound interest functions as geometric wealth accumulation:
A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)]. Systematic monthly index dollar-cost averaging outpaces non-compounding cash reserves by geometric orders of magnitude over 10+ year time horizons.
